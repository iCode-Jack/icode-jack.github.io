ExamMocker Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how the app ExamMocker processes information. ExamMocker is a structured mock-exam practice app that works with digital exam packages.

2. Account information

To use certain features, you may create an account. Account-related data may include the information you provide during registration and data needed to maintain your account and entitlements, such as the status of your exam access.

3. Sign in with Apple

If you choose Sign in with Apple, Apple provides limited account information to the app to create or link your account, in accordance with the sharing options you select in Apple's sign-in interface.

4. Email authentication

If you sign in with email, the email address you provide is used for authentication and account-related communications, such as verification messages. Please use an email address you can access.

5. Exam and .mock data

Imported exam packages and your practice activity are used to provide the app's study functionality, such as displaying exam content and tracking your progress within the app.

6. Cloud services

The app uses cloud services to support account and entitlement-related functionality. Account and related app data transmitted to these services is used to operate the app and is handled in accordance with the applicable service providers' terms and policies.

7. Advertising, if applicable

If advertising is shown in the app, advertising services may process certain device and advertising-related information. Any consent choices required by applicable law are presented in the app.

8. Data retention

Account-related information is retained while your account remains active and as needed to provide the app's functionality. You may request deletion of your account as described below.

9. Account deletion

You may request deletion of your ExamMocker account and associated account data by contacting the developer through the support channels listed on this site. Deletion requests will be handled within a reasonable time, subject to applicable legal and record-keeping requirements.

10. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect account information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so protecting your sign-in credentials and keeping your device updated also helps protect your account.

11. Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through this app. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact the developer so it can be addressed.

12. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date on this page.
