Photo & Video Toolkit Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how the app Photo & Video Toolkit processes information. The app is an offline-first utility designed for cropping, resizing, compressing, and optimizing photos and videos.

2. Information processed by the app

The app requires no account and no sign-in. The developer does not operate a user account system for this app, and does not ask you to provide identifying information to use its core features.

3. Photos and videos

Photos and videos you choose to edit are primarily processed locally on your device rather than uploaded to developer-operated storage. The app does not include a developer-operated cloud photo or video storage backend. If you delete the app or its data from your device, the locally stored app data is removed from the device.

4. Advertising

The app may display advertising provided through Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Advertising services may process certain device and advertising-related information, such as advertising identifiers, device information, and usage or interaction data related to ads shown in the app.

5. Consent management

Where required by applicable law, the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to present consent and privacy choices related to advertising. Your selections in the consent interface are stored and applied in accordance with applicable requirements.

6. Third-party services

The app's advertising relies on third-party advertising services. Google's handling of information is described in its own privacy policy:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

7. Data retention

The developer does not maintain a server-side user data store for this app's media. Advertising-related data processed by third parties is retained according to their own policies.

8. Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through this app.

9. Security

Because media is primarily processed locally on your device, protecting your device with a passcode, encryption where available, and timely software updates helps protect your content.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date on this page.
